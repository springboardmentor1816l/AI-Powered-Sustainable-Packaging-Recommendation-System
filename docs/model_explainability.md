\### Cost Model – Feature Importance

The cost prediction model is primarily driven by the total material weight used, indicating that material consumption is the dominant cost factor. Product category indicators contribute marginally, reflecting category-specific cost variations. Composite indices such as MSS have limited influence, improving the interpretability of the model by emphasizing direct physical and business drivers of cost.



\### CO₂ Model – Feature Importance

The CO₂ emission model is strongly influenced by normalized product weight, followed by fragility and annual usage. This aligns with real-world emission behavior, where heavier and frequently used products generate higher emissions. Sustainability indices contribute minimally, suggesting that emissions are largely governed by physical material characteristics rather than abstract sustainability scores.
